tngld privacy policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

tngld is a planning app for couples. It's made by FSY Consulting Inc. This page explains what tngld keeps, why, who else handles it, and how to delete it. Questions go to app.tngld@gmail.com.

The short version

  • tngld keeps what you put in it so the two of you can share it. That's all it's for.
  • Only you and the partner who joins your household can see your household.
  • No ads, no tracking across other apps, and we never sell your data.
  • You can delete your account from inside the app at any time.

What tngld keeps

Your sign-in. When you continue with Apple, Apple gives tngld an ID for you and an email address. That may be a private relay address if you choose to hide your email. tngld uses it only to sign you in.

What you tell it during setup:

  • your first name
  • whether you have kids, and their first names if you add them
  • your city
  • your time zone, taken from your phone

What the two of you add. This covers your plans, chores, dinners, groceries, board cards and notes. It also covers your energy check-ins, your weekly check-ins and who is doing what. tngld also keeps a short activity history, such as "Alex moved swimming to Thursday", so each of you can see what the other changed.

Your photo, if you add one.

Busy times, if you connect a calendar (tngld Pro). Only when you're busy (start and end) and which of your calendars it came from, so both of you can see it in Today. Never event titles, details, places or who's invited. You choose the calendars, on your own phone, and can turn them off at any time.

Your reminder settings, and a push token for your phone. The push token lets tngld send reminders to that phone.

Feedback you send. When you use Send feedback (or shake a beta build), tngld keeps what you wrote, your app version, your iPhone model and iOS version, and a screenshot if you chose to include one. Only the people who make tngld can read it, to fix problems and improve the app.

How the app is used, as counts. tngld records that things happened, such as "a plan was added", "a check-in was done" or "the app was opened today", with a random ID for you and for your household. It never records titles, notes, names, places, or anything you type. This tells us which features help and where tngld gets in the way. It's never used for advertising, and it can be switched off for everyone at any time.

tngld doesn't collect your location, contacts, health data or browsing activity, and it doesn't use advertising IDs.

Who can see it

Your household is visible only to the people in it: you, and the partner who joins with your 6-letter code. The database enforces this, not just the app. The people who run tngld can access the database to keep it working and to fix problems, and don't look at households otherwise.

Other companies that handle it

tngld uses a few services to run. Each only gets what it needs:

  • Supabase: stores your household and photos, and signs you in. Hosted in the United States (Amazon Web Services, US East).
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, push notifications, and TestFlight during the beta.
  • Expo: passes reminders to Apple to deliver to your phone. Expo sees the text of the reminder.
  • PostHog: receives the usage counts described above (event names and random IDs, with your app version, iPhone model, iOS version and language), never what you write. Hosted in the United States. It doesn't keep your IP address, and doesn't record your screen or your taps.
  • Anthropic (Claude): tngld Pro features use Claude, Anthropic's AI, to write suggestions: the Week draft, Today's outlook, "What can I take?", the monthly recap, Quick add, meal icons and ingredient ideas, and outing ideas. They only run if someone in your household turns on Suggestions from Claude in Settings, and either of you can turn it off at any time.
    • What's sent: a summary of your plans, routines, check-ins, meals and groceries: first names (yours, and your kids' first names), plan and routine titles, times, check-in levels, meal names, place names such as your city, and patterns from past weeks. If you connect a calendar, only busy or free times are used, never event titles or details.
    • What's never sent: your notes, email addresses, exact locations, or anything about your kids beyond their first names.
    • Voice: when you hold the mic or share a voice note, your iPhone turns the speech into text on the phone itself. The audio never leaves your iPhone and isn't kept; only the text is used, like anything you type.
    • Checked and confirmed: every suggestion is checked against your real plans before you see it, and nothing changes until one of you taps it.
    • Retention: Anthropic doesn't use data sent this way to train its models. It deletes what tngld sends, and what Claude writes back, within 30 days. The Saturday Week draft and the monthly recap are sent in a batch, whose results Anthropic keeps for 29 days. Anthropic may keep data longer only where the law requires it, or for up to 2 years if its safety systems flag a request as breaking its usage policy.
    • What tngld keeps: counts of how much each feature is used (tokens and cost), to keep costs under a monthly cap. Never the text that was sent or written.

How long it's kept

tngld keeps your household for as long as you use it.

When you delete your account, your sign-in, name, photo and reminder settings are removed right away. Whether the household goes too depends on who is left:

  • If your partner is still in the household, the shared plans stay with them.
  • If you were the last one in it, the whole household is deleted.

There are no separate backup copies: on tngld's current database plan, nothing deleted is kept anywhere else. If that changes (for example, daily backups kept for 7 days), this page will say so first.

Deleting your account

Go to Settings → You → Delete account. It asks once more, then deletes everything described above. You can also email app.tngld@gmail.com and we'll do it for you.

Children

tngld is for adults. Parents may add their children's first names so plans can mention them. tngld doesn't collect anything else about children, and children shouldn't have accounts.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll say so in the app before it takes effect.
